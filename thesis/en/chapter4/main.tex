\section{Experimental Setup and Evaluation Protocol}
\label{sec:exp_environment}

I conducted all training and ablation experiments on a cloud server equipped with an NVIDIA A100-SXM4-80GB GPU and 167 GiB of system RAM (Table~\ref{tab:hardware_software_env}). Following standard VRD benchmarks \cite{huang2022layoutlmv3}, I evaluate performance using strict entity-level Precision ($P$), Recall ($R$), and micro-F1 computed via \texttt{seqeval} under the IOB2 scheme, where a predicted span is counted as a True Positive only if both its exact token boundaries $(i_{\text{start}}, i_{\text{end}})$ and semantic category $c_k$ match the ground truth. To account for optimization variance, every model configuration is trained and evaluated independently across three random seeds $\mathcal{S} = \{42, 123, 1993\}$, and all tables report $\text{Mean} \pm \text{Std}$.

\section{Benchmark Datasets and Hyperparameters}
\label{sec:exp_datasets}

I evaluate my framework on two canonical Document AI benchmarks representing distinct layout regimes (Table~\ref{tab:dataset_statistics}):
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{FUNSD} \cite{jaume2019funsd}: Comprises 199 noisy scanned administrative and questionnaire forms (149 train, 50 test) with 9,707 entities and 31,485 words extracted from RVL-CDIP. Active entities are categorized into \texttt{HEADER} ($11.8\%$), \texttt{QUESTION} ($44.2\%$), and \texttt{ANSWER} ($33.1\%$), alongside background \texttt{OTHER} ($10.9\%$), yielding $|\mathcal{Y}| = 7$ IOB2 tags. FUNSD tests multi-column layout disambiguation, multi-line answer consistency, and inline key-value separation.
    \item \textbf{CORD} \cite{park2019cord}: Contains 1,000 Indonesian retail and restaurant receipts (800 train, 100 validation, 100 test) captured under mobile camera distortion, thermal fading, and watermark clutter. It defines 30 fine-grained classes across 5 super-categories (\texttt{menu.*}, \texttt{sub\_total.*}, \texttt{total.*}, \texttt{void\_menu.*}, \texttt{etc.*}), yielding $|\mathcal{Y}| = 61$ IOB2 tags.
\end{itemize}

Three key findings emerge from Table~\ref{tab:main_results}:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Substantial Gains on Complex Form Layouts (FUNSD):}
    My Full 4-Module Architecture elevates entity-level F1 from $89.92 \pm 0.85\%$ to $\mathbf{92.12 \pm 0.14\%}$ on FUNSD \texttt{base} ($\mathbf{+2.20\%}$ over baseline) and from $91.24 \pm 0.61\%$ to $\mathbf{93.35 \pm 0.18\%}$ on FUNSD \texttt{large} ($\mathbf{+2.11\%}$ over baseline). Simultaneously, cross-seed standard deviation drops by $6.1\times$ on \texttt{base} ($0.85\% \to 0.14\%$) and $3.4\times$ on \texttt{large} ($0.61\% \to 0.18\%$).
    \item \textbf{Consistent Improvements on High-Baseline Receipts (CORD):}
    Even though vanilla LayoutLMv3 already exceeds $96.7\%$ F1 on CORD, my Full 4-Module Model pushes F1 to $\mathbf{97.28 \pm 0.12\%}$ on \texttt{base} ($\mathbf{+0.56\%}$ over baseline) and $\mathbf{97.62 \pm 0.08\%}$ on \texttt{large} ($\mathbf{+0.47\%}$ over baseline), reducing relative entity error rates by $17.1\%$ and $16.5\%$, respectively.
    \item \textbf{Impact of Contrastive Calibration:}
    Whereas the initial uncalibrated post-SCM contrastive formulation suffered from gradient conflict on \texttt{base} ($-0.33\%$ on FUNSD \texttt{base} and $-0.36\%$ on CORD \texttt{base} vs.\ the 3-module model), decoupling contrastive alignment to pre-SCM token states $\mathbf{h}_i$ with detached visual patch anchors and spatial-overlap soft targets yields positive gains over the 3-module model across every benchmark: $\mathbf{+0.54\%}$ on FUNSD \texttt{base}, $\mathbf{+0.90\%}$ on FUNSD \texttt{large}, $\mathbf{+0.37\%}$ on CORD \texttt{base}, and $\mathbf{+0.27\%}$ on CORD \texttt{large}.
\end{enumerate}

Figure~\ref{fig:module_impact_chart} and Table~\ref{tab:master_ablation_matrix} establish four core architectural conclusions:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{SCM Has the Greatest Performance Impact:}
    Across every dataset and backbone scale, removing the Segment Context Module triggers the largest F1 drop relative to the Full Model: $\mathbf{-2.34\%}$ on FUNSD \texttt{large} ($93.35\% \to 91.01\%$), $\mathbf{-1.76\%}$ on FUNSD \texttt{base} ($92.12\% \to 90.36\%$), $\mathbf{-0.48\%}$ on CORD \texttt{base}, and $\mathbf{-0.40\%}$ on CORD \texttt{large}. Pooling subwords into line segments and propagating inter-segment context via ReZero gating is the primary driver of multi-word entity consistency and key-value binding.
    \item \textbf{Intra-Line Boundary Supervision Prevents Span Mergers:}
    Removing $\mathcal{L}_{\text{bound}}$ causes the second-largest drop on FUNSD ($\mathbf{-1.20\%}$ on \texttt{large} and $\mathbf{-1.00\%}$ on \texttt{base}) and a $\mathbf{-0.30\%}$ drop on CORD, confirming that explicit pairwise transition supervision is critical for separating adjacent inline keys and values.
    \item \textbf{Hierarchical Position Encoding Resolves Multi-Column Ambiguity:}
    Removing HPE reduces F1 by $\mathbf{-1.00\%}$ on FUNSD \texttt{large}, $\mathbf{-0.79\%}$ on FUNSD \texttt{base}, and $\mathbf{-0.24\%}$ on CORD, demonstrating that explicit line, column, and block indices provide structural inductive bias that raw 2D coordinates lack.
    \item \textbf{Calibrated Contrastive Alignment Enhances Visual Grounding:}
    Omitting the calibrated $\mathcal{L}_{\text{cont}}$ reduces F1 by $\mathbf{-0.90\%}$ on FUNSD \texttt{large}, $\mathbf{-0.54\%}$ on FUNSD \texttt{base}, $\mathbf{-0.37\%}$ on CORD \texttt{base}, and $\mathbf{-0.27\%}$ on CORD \texttt{large}, verifying that pre-SCM soft-target alignment with detached visual anchors effectively suppresses background watermark and margin noise.
\end{enumerate}
